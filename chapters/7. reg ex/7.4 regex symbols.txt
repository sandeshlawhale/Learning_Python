symbols             meanings    
?                   matches zero or one
*                   matches zero or more
+                   matches one or more
{n}                 matches exactly n o f the preceding group  ex. (ha){3} -> hahaha
{n,}                matches n or more
{,m}                matches zero to m
{n,m}               matches atleast n to atmost m 
{n,m}? | *? | +?    performs a non greedy matches
^spam               ensures the string should starts with spam
spam$               ensures the string ends with spam
.                   matches any character except new line (\n)
\d, \w, \s          matches digits, word and spaces
\D, \W, \s          matches everything excep digits, words, and spaces or newline
[abc]               matches any character that is in between brackets
[^abc]              matches any character that is not in between brackets
